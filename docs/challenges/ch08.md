# Challenge 08 — Field Device Recovery

**Points:** 350 · **Difficulty:** Hard · **Type:** Defensive · **Surface:** SNMP (UDP 161)

> Spoiler-free. This guide explains the *surface* and the *idea* — not the answer.
> This is a **defensive / recovery** challenge: you restore a tampered device, you don't break one.

## The Story

A field **RTU** is running a tampered firmware image. Your job is incident response: confirm it's
unhealthy, and restore it to its last-good image over **SNMP** — the protocol real operators use to
manage field devices.

## Where to start

- The RTU's SNMP agent is on **UDP 161**.
- The recovery **runbook is an evidence file** — the `rtu07_recovery` attachment, linked from the
  Record Findings page (`http://10.0.1.1/investigate`) and the in-scenario mail.
- The Record Findings page shows the RTU's **live health** so you can watch it change.

## Objectives

1. Query the RTU over SNMP and read its status.
2. Confirm it's unhealthy (running image ≠ last-good image).
3. Enter recovery mode.
4. Restore the last-good image and verify it reads **healthy**.

## Hints

<details><summary>1. How do I talk SNMP here?</summary>

Standard SNMP tooling works (`snmpget`/`snmpwalk`, or any SNMP library). Walk the agent to find the
OIDs that report image status and the ones that drive recovery.
</details>

<details><summary>2. What order do the steps go in?</summary>

The runbook (`rtu07_recovery`) lays out the recovery sequence. Follow it — recovery usually means
entering a mode first, then writing the restore action, then verifying.
</details>

<details><summary>3. How do I know I'm done?</summary>

The device must *read healthy* (running image == last-good). The Record Findings page shows live status;
when it flips to healthy and holds, the badge awards it.
</details>

## What you'll learn

- How SNMP is used to inspect and manage field devices.
- Firmware-integrity concepts (running vs. last-good image).
- A structured incident-recovery workflow.

## Scoreboard

Awarded when the RTU is restored and verified healthy.
